Frequently asked questions

How do I fund my wallet?

After you create an account you receive a dedicated virtual account number. Transfer any amount from ₦100 upward and your wallet balance updates automatically.

When am I charged for a verification?

The fee is shown on a confirmation screen before anything happens. Your wallet is only debited after you choose Confirm & Verify.

What is the difference between Basic, Standard and Premium?

Basic confirms core identity details. Standard adds location and contact details. Premium produces the most detailed report layout.

Can I withdraw money from my wallet?

No. Wallet funds can only be used for verification services on the platform.

Can I download my verification result?

Yes. Every result can be downloaded as a PDF or printed, and stays available in your verification history.
